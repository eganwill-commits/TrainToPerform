# Train to Perform — Pricing & Program Policies *Draft for review — v1.0* --- ## About the Program Train to Perform (T2P) is a progressive strength and athletic development system built for teen athletes. Every program is structured around five training pillars — **MVT** (Movement), **PWR** (Power), **STR** (Strength), **SKL** (Skill), and **FIN** (Finisher/Conditioning) — and organized into 4-week blocks with testing weeks built in to measure real progress. Sessions run **1 hour 45 minutes** and are led by a CrossFit Level 1 certified coach. Small group training is capped at **6 athletes** to protect coaching quality and individual attention. --- ## Program Offerings ### Summer Performance Block *(Primary Offering)* A 12-week off-season athletic development program designed to replace or supplement seasonal sport. Three 4-week blocks of progressive training, with testing weeks at the end of each block to measure and document gains. - **Duration:** 12 weeks - **Frequency:** 3 sessions per week - **Session length:** 1:45 - **Group size:** 5–6 athletes - **Includes:** Full T2P 5-pillar programming, baseline and progress testing, block-to-block progression, group accountability, written program access **Pricing:** - $650 / month (3 monthly payments) - $1,750 paid in full at program start *(save $200)* --- ### In-Season Maintenance Designed to keep athletes strong, durable, and injury-resistant during their competitive sport season without compromising recovery. - **Frequency:** 2 sessions per week - **Session length:** 1:45 - **Pricing:** $300 / month --- ### Private 1-on-1 Individualized programming built inside the T2P framework with sport-specific customization, weekly check-ins between sessions, and full written program access. - **Onboarding & assessment:** $200 *(one-time, includes movement screen and baseline testing)* - **Single session (75 min):** $150 - **12-session pack:** $1,650 *($137.50/session — one on us)* --- ### Hybrid Package *(Recommended for serious athletes)* Combines individual attention with the energy of group training. - **Structure:** 1 private session + 2 group sessions per week - **Pricing:** $600 / month - **Best for:** Athletes returning from injury, college-prep athletes, or anyone wanting to accelerate a specific weakness --- ## Policies ### Pause & Travel Policy Families can pause membership for travel without losing program time. - **Allowance:** One pause per athlete per Summer Block - **Minimum pause length:** 2 weeks - **Notice required:** 7 days in advance - **How it works:** Billing pauses during the absence and resumes when the athlete returns. The program extends by the pause length so no training time is lost. **Weekly billing is not offered.** Progressive programming depends on consistent attendance — pricing per week breaks the block system and compromises results. ### Missed Sessions (short absences under 2 weeks) - Athletes may attend an additional session in the same week to replace one missed, space permitting. - Up to 2 unused sessions may be rolled into the following month. - No pro-rating or refunds for missed sessions within a billing month. ### Payment Terms - Monthly billing runs from the athlete's program start date. - Accepted methods: *[to be confirmed — credit card, ACH, Venmo Business]* - Paid-in-full discounts apply only at program start. - Returned payments incur a $25 fee. ### Cancellation - **Summer Performance Block:** Non-refundable once Block 1 begins. Full refund if cancelled before first session. 50% refund if cancelled during Week 1. - **Monthly continuity (in-season, hybrid, private):** 30 days written notice to cancel. Current month's payment is non-refundable. ### Rate Review Rates are reviewed annually each May. Existing families receive 60 days' notice of any changes and a 30-day window to lock in current pricing for the upcoming block. --- ## Onboarding Process 1. **Inquiry call** — 15-minute conversation to understand the athlete's sport, goals, injury history, and training background. 2. **Assessment session** ($200, applied as credit toward first month for 1-on-1 clients) — movement screen, baseline testing across the 5 pillars, written recommendation for the right program fit. 3. **Program enrollment** — placement into the appropriate track (Summer Block, In-Season, Private, or Hybrid). 4. **Kickoff** — first training session + parent briefing on expectations, tracking, and communication. --- ## What's Not Included To protect the integrity and pricing of the program, the following are *not* part of standard membership: - Nutrition programming *(available as add-on consult)* - Sport-specific skill coaching *(SKL pillar covers general athleticism, not sport technique)* - Physical therapy or rehabilitation *(referrals available)* - Tournament/competition travel coaching --- *Questions or to enroll: [contact info] | traintoperform.fit*
